## What I'm Building

### Agentic AI & Generative Engineering

I focus on architecting autonomous AI systems — not just prompting, but orchestrating AI components to perform real work:

- **Multi-Agent Architectures** — Designing systems where AI workers collaborate, delegate, and maintain state
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — Connecting LLMs to local files, APIs, and tools so they can act, not just respond
- **Low-Code Integration** — Using Azure Logic Apps as the orchestration layer for AI workflows
- **Local & Open Source Models** — Experimenting with Qwen, Gemini Flash, and other models for specialized tasks

### Cloud Engineering & Infrastructure as Code

Building and deploying complex environments with automation-first principles:

- **IaC Mastery** — Azure Bicep & Terraform for reproducible, testable infrastructure
- **Azure Virtual Desktop** — Automated deployments with Terraform
- **Cost Optimization** — Tools for SPOT pricing, resource cleanup, and efficient cloud spend
- **Self-Hosted Kubernetes** — Alternative approaches beyond managed services

### Energy Transition & Home Automation

A deep technical interest in smart grids and the Dutch energy market:

- **Home Battery Systems** — Victron inverters, LFP batteries, Power Assist for load management
- **Dynamic Pricing Automation** — Systems that charge when prices are negative, discharge when expensive
- **Grid Congestion Analysis** — Tracking netcongestie and capacity issues in the Netherlands
